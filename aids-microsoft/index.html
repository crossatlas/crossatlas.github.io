About this atlas

A parallel timeline of the AIDS epidemic and Microsoft, 1975 to today. The two stories effectively start two months apart: the CDC's first report on the epidemic ran June 5, 1981, and the IBM PC shipped with MS-DOS that August. They run on opposite trajectories through the 1990s and converge in 2000, when the Gates Foundation begins turning Microsoft wealth into one of the largest private funding streams for HIV/AIDS work in history.

Reading it

The top band is the epidemic, the bottom band is Microsoft, and the green diamonds between them are where the stories meet. Each dot is dated to the month where one applies. Drag the slider or press play to replay history; events ahead of the current year fade out. Hover or tap a dot for the full story, click it to jump there.

Data

US deaths curve: approximate deaths per year with HIV disease as cause, from CDC HIV surveillance reports, rounded. Microsoft revenue: approximate annual revenue by fiscal year from Microsoft's public annual reports, plotted on a log scale. Both series are shown side by side for orientation; nothing here implies one caused the other.
