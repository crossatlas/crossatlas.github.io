Arrows point from the country people left to where they live; thicker arrows mean more people. Growth: people from low- and middle-income countries living in high-income countries nearly tripled from 48.5 million in 1990 to 143.3 million in 2024, about 3.2% more each year, compounding (doubling roughly every 22 years). Worldwide, migrants went from 2.9% to 3.7% of all people.

Source: UN DESA International Migrant Stock 2024, via Our World in Data. Income groups: World Bank (current classification). Work migration view: World Bank KNOMAD bilateral remittance estimates, 2021 (money migrant workers send home; arrows still point from home country to where they work), and the ILO's 2022 count of migrant workers. No source counts workers by origin and destination worldwide, so remittances are the closest measure. People view: figures count people living outside their country of birth in each year, including refugees, not the number who moved that year. The UN publishes every five years (1990 to 2020, then 2024); years in between are estimated on a straight line.
